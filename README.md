# METALUXE — Engineering & Fabrications (UAE)

**Demo:** https://metaluxe-website.pages.dev/

## About
METALUXE is a steel fabrications and machining works business established in **1992**. Primary focus includes **turning and machining works** for **industrial, marine, irrigation, onshore & offshore** needs. 

**Mission:** Provide quality fabrication, installation, and erection of structural steel & process equipment at a fair and competitive price. 

**Note on current site:** “M/S. CRESCENT GIFTS & DESIGNING EST is ADNOC ICV Certified (Certificate ID-107914)”. 

## Core services
- Steel Fabrication & Turney 
- Milling 
- Lathe Works & Mechanical Services 
- Casting Works 
- CNC Production 

## Product categories (from current site)
Hydraulic fittings, oilfield fittings, machinery parts, shaft coupling, nylon rollers, pipe threading, PU & rubber end caps, o-rings, gaskets, springs, steel gate & grill, metal casting, foundry works, safety signs & structural sign boards, orifice plate, EPDM hose tube, gears, calibration scales.
